Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data a website keeps in your browser. This page explains how oscolderst.github.io uses them.

1. The site’s own storage

The site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising tools. It keeps only two items in your browser’s local storage:

NamePurposeDurationType
oscolderst_age_okRemembers that you confirmed you are 18 or over, so the age notice is not shown again.Until you delete itNecessary
oscolderst_twitch_okRemembers that you chose to load the Twitch player, so it loads directly on your next visit.Until you delete itPreference

2. Third-party cookies

Some pages show content from other services, which may set cookies on their own domains. These cookies are controlled by those services, not by oscolderst:

  • Twitch — the stream player on the Home and Live pages. It only loads after you click “Watch stream”, so no Twitch player cookies are set until you choose to watch. See the Twitch privacy notice.
  • bonushunt.gg — the bonus hunt widget on the Home and Live pages, which loads when the page opens.

When you click an offer link, the operator’s site may set an affiliate cookie to record that you came from here. This happens on their site and is covered by their own cookie policy.

3. Managing cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking third-party cookies may stop the Twitch player or the bonus hunt widget from working. Clearing this site’s data will show the age notice again and the Twitch player will ask before loading.
